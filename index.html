Murray Robertson

Composer Sound Designer Interactive Artist

As a composer, my focus lies on weaving sound into interactive experiences. I am inspired by field recording, sound design, and using recognizable sounds in unexpected places. I have a deep passion designing technology for interfacing with sound (like at the top of this page), and I believe there is a richer musical experience when audiences participate in what they hear. Whether the medium be performances, video games, web design, or fixed media, my music aims to explore this world where sound is responsive, evokes strong emotion, and enhances its surroundings.
