I Will See You Later

For Big Band

I wrote this piece in 2024 for the Estonian Academy of Music and Theatre's Large Jazz Ensemble "Bigjazzband," expressing the intimate emotion in leaving something behind temporarily. I focused on the harmonic color possible to achieve with EAMT's diverse instrumentation: strings, reeds, and myself on tuba, as well as a huge rhythm section with 2 guitars, a bass, keyboard... I was very proud to make the version for "standard" big band 6 months later for Capital University's Big Band and premier it at the 2025 NOW Music Festival.

This piece is dedicated to my teacher Stan Smith. He and I spent a long time discussing the worlds of harmony possible when combining methods from jazz, classical, and contemporary musicians' theories.
