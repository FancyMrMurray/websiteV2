Rock Salt Hurts

For Big Band

I wrote this piece in 2024 for the Estonian Academy of Music and Theatre's Large Jazz Ensemble "Bigjazzband," and since have written out a version for standard big band. When I landed in Estonia in the middle of Northern European winter - I found out quickly that falling on the ice (on all the rock salt...) is not pleasant. When I was tasked with writing my first piece for the ensemble this was something still lingering in my mind. Using modal chord progressions and a time signature change over the bridge, I wanted to create a cohesive groove. Many thanks to Raul Sööt and the EMTA BigJazzBand for premiering this piece in 2024.

If you would like to perform this piece, please email me at mwrobertson02@gmail.com and I will send you the parts (for either version - big band or "bigjazzband") :)
