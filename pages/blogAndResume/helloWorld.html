Hello, World!

My website is finally here...

June 08, 2026

Ok, so I just wanted to say a few words (cause I can cause this is just a blank infinite** digital canvas) about like the web. And sound art.

I really really love the web. I also, as a “Gen Z” kinda hate it? But not really. Like I love the internet. Totally and wholly. It raised me(??) in part thanks to the Nintendo Wii and Xbox live, minecraft servers, the rise of instagram, tiktok, etc., and most of all people sharing their art on the internet. The internet is where I learned that writing crazy-ass music is possible and it is where I got my sea legs in terms of moving from a band kid to being a full fledged musician. To be clear: it was the actual training and the lessons and stuff. Shoutout Tom and Tony for teaching me the tuba. But like the internet was definitely a reason I got realllllly excited about all that stuff.

This webpage is kinda like my own way of taking some of that back. From the tech companies that invented the infinite scroll and then just kinda. Mutually Assured Destroyed everything in the process.

Art on the internet is something extremely important to me- but we (human beings) (who like to make art) (and hopefully like consuming really good art) need to like take a breath and just start being really intentional about our art. We gotta stop making slop when literally every single person alive has the capacity to do at least one (1) intentional art at some point in their life.

I hope that this website is sort of that- a place for my web based interactive stuff to live, a place where I have control over the font and the spacing and the margins and the … (wait hold on lemme fix that formatting error) … the word-breaks and every character of HTML in the background. Because I kinda long for the days when you would like. Put HTML in stuff cause it was easy.

This isn’t a crazy advanced thing to learn how to do and I hope every artist in every field takes a stab at least once at creative code. Even if it's just to take their square inch of the internet back from LinkedIn and social media sites. Make your presence your own - not Zuckerberg’s, Elon’s, or Gates’.

I don’t care. My phone mic is already listening. I want my custom font and my colors and my background images and my javascript).
