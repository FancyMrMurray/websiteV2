Hi!

My name is Murray Robertson, I am a composer and sound designer whose artistic practice is centered on a synthesis of real-world and virtual sonic elements. I am passionate about interactive art and interactive music designs; particularly the way that sound can both augment and be augmented by a user's interaction. I combine computer code, live performance, and traditional composing/producing to saturate digital spaces with sound.

My love for interactive sound designs can be seen across this website where I have built and embedded multiple components of interactive sound. On my homepage, there is a granular synthesis engine playing portfolio examples when you mouse near my subtitles (right at the top), and my graduate capstone “TryAllYouWant,” an in-browser sound-only exploration experience, can be found under MurrayRobertson\\InteractiveArtist. I used cycling74’s RNBO language to write the sound synthesis and vanilla JavaScript for the interfacing and browser functionality.

I grew up playing tuba and trombone in central Ohio, just outside the Hocking Hills. Moving to Columbus to study at Capital University, I had the opportunity to go abroad to Tallinn, Estonia and start my practice in electro-acoustic music and interactive art at the Estonian Academy of Music and Theater. Moving on from Ohio, I studied interactive sound design and production at Northwestern University’s School of Communication in the Sound Arts and Industries program. Now, living in Chicago, my skills are brought into practice through my fluency with both technology and live performance.

Feel free to explore my site or contact me directly at mwrobertson02@gmail.com for any questions about my sound art, music compositions, web designs, or music performance.
